# MTS Expanse

> Start on a tiny island. Feed the hungry chests. Earn every cell of land.

[![Discord](https://img.shields.io/badge/Discord-join%20the%20server-5865F2?logo=discord&logoColor=white)](https://discord.gg/tWz4FT74pH) [![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-source-181717?logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/zzh8829/mts-expanse)

MTS Expanse makes map expansion the game. Your team starts on a small patch of land with nothing around it, and the blue chests on its edge are hungry: deliver what one asks for and the land behind it opens. It is a mod port of the Expanse scenario from [ComfyFactorio](https://github.com/ComfyFactory/ComfyFactorio), rebuilt so that every team on a [Multi-Team Support](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/multi-team-support) server gets its own island and its own progress. It plays solo and co-op without Multi-Team Support too.

## Status

Playable and in active development. Version 0.1.25 and newer need Factorio 2.1; 0.1.24 is the last version for Factorio 2.0. A save from 2.0 loads on 2.1 and keeps its land, missions and research. Report anything odd on [Discord](https://discord.gg/tWz4FT74pH) or in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/zzh8829/mts-expanse/issues).

## Quick start

1. Enable MTS Expanse. For teams, keep [Multi-Team Support](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/multi-team-support) enabled as well; the game suggests it.
2. Start a new game. Every team begins on its own tiny island.
3. Open a blue hungry chest on the edge of your land to see what it wants. Deliver it and the cell behind it opens.
4. A request you cannot afford yet? Put a coin in the chest and it asks for something else.
5. With Space Age, open Space missions (rocket icon, top left) once your team finds its first rocket silo.

## Features

Land

- Every cell of land is bought from a hungry chest. Requests and terrain grow with the distance from spawn.
- The tree and the rock at spawn never run out, so wood, ore and stone are always there.
- Red warning signs mark where biters may land, and an invasion is announced ahead of time.

Teams

- With Multi-Team Support every team has its own surface, chests, missions, statistics and invasion timers.
- Cell content can be the same for every team: the same coordinates give the same terrain and the same requests, so a race is decided by play.

Space Age

- Rocket silos are found, not built. Seven ships ask for cargo and send resources from other planets to your landing pad in return.
- A cargo chest beside each silo requests what the mission still needs, and the rocket is loaded and launched for you.
- A mission board, hover tooltips, map pins and alerts say what each ship needs and where its silo is.
- Vulcanus, Fulgora, Gleba and Aquilo cells appear in the outer ring of your own surface.

## Compatibility

Requires Factorio 2.1. [Multi-Team Support](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/multi-team-support) 0.7.0 or newer is recommended and optional. Space Age is optional and detected automatically; without it Expanse plays in vanilla mode with ordinary rocket silos. Quality is optional as well: without it a mission that asks for a higher quality asks for more normal items.

## Works with

- [Multi-Team Support](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/multi-team-support): one server, one seed, a private island for every team.
- [MTS Dimension Warp](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/mts-dimension-warp): another whole game for MTS teams, where every team warps its own base.
- [Brave New MTS](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/brave-new-mts): remote-only, character-free play, also built on MTS.
